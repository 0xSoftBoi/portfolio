An evaluation of LLM-assisted vulnerability detection that turned into research on contamination, evaluator validity, and benchmark construction.

Scientific ML

active-materials-discovery

Active learning on pretrained universal materials models, with MC-dropout calibration analysis and optimized batched inference.

~5× discovery acceleration; uncertainty was anti-correlated with error and not useful as the presumed acquisition signal.
