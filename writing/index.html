Editorial rule

Evidence has to appear on the page.

Primary sources before secondary summaries. Quantified claims expose denominator, units, assumptions, and uncertainty. The strongest rival explanation gets a fair test. Facts, derivations, estimates, and targets are labeled as different evidence classes. If a piece cannot survive those gates, it remains a note or draft rather than being promoted to research.
